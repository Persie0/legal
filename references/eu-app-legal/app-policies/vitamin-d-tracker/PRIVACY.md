# Privacy Policy – Vitamin D Tracker

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Who does this policy apply to?

This policy applies to **Vitamin D Tracker**, an application for keeping vitamin D and sun exposure records with optional weather/UV queries. Health information may constitute specially protected data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR; a separate [consent declaration](https://github.com/Persie0/legal/blob/main/references/eu-app-legal/app-policies/GESUNDHEITSEINWILLIGUNG_VITAMIN_D.md) applies to this. A general app EULA click alone cannot replace the required consent. For external websites or voluntarily connected online accounts, the operators' own privacy notices may additionally apply.

## 3. At a glance

- Your vitamin D, supplement, sun exposure and blood test records are stored locally on your device; no user account is required.
- For health-related processing that goes beyond keeping the app locally, an express consent under Art. 9 GDPR applies.
- Optional: UV/weather values via OpenWeather with your own API key, backup/export via Google Drive, Pro unlockings via the app store (verification via RevenueCat).
- Error reports go to a self-operated GlitchTip installation in Frankfurt am Main.

## 4. Processing by purpose

We distinguish between information processed exclusively in the private storage of your device and information that arises with network services. Signing in to an app store does not automatically mean that Persie0 receives all data stored by that store.

**Local app function**

- Data: vitamin D estimates and history; supplements, sun exposure, skin type and details of the exposed body area; blood test values; reminders, settings and the OpenWeather API key.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. For the evaluation of health-related information insofar as it goes beyond local documentation, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR through express consent.
- Recipients: none; the processing takes place on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete it, reset the app data or uninstall.

**Enable payment features, restore purchases, prevent fraud and provide support** *(optional)*

- Data: pseudonymous/anonymous app user identifier, product and entitlement identifiers, purchase/subscription status, store transaction metadata, technical app/device data and the IP visible in network logs. Persie0 receives no payment card data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (purchase/service) and Art. 6(1)(f) (fraud prevention and reliable entitlement status).
- Recipients: RevenueCat and the selected app store; the store is independently responsible for payment (see section 6).
- Retention: for the purchase/subscription relationship and as necessary for restoration, disputes and statutory bookkeeping/claim periods; thereafter deletion or de-identification.

**Providing local UV and weather values** *(optional, at your request)*

- Data: exact latitude/longitude, IP/request metadata, time and your OpenWeather API key.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for health-related use, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR through express consent. The location permission alone is not consent to other purposes.
- Recipients: OpenWeather according to its account/API terms (see section 6).
- Retention: Persie0 does not store the request on a server; local results/histories remain until you delete them. OpenWeather stores request/account logs according to its policy.

**Choosing regional defaults, currency, language or the consent flow**

- Data: public IP address, request metadata and a country/region derived from them; no GPS location.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (minimal regional configuration). Regional settings can be overridden where offered.
- Recipients: whichever country-lookup service is available first (see section 6).
- Retention: the response is used transiently or cached locally; Persie0 keeps no server log. Providers store request logs according to their policies.

**Backing up, restoring and exporting your data on request** *(optional)*

- Data: Google account authorisation and access to Drive app data; database backup, settings and generated table/export summaries with possible vitamin D, supplement, sun exposure and blood test data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a)/(b) GDPR and, for health-related data, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR through express consent. Synchronisation is optional; withdrawal by disconnecting.
- Recipients: Google; the data is stored in the Google Drive account you select (see section 6).
- Retention: until you delete the backup/export in Drive, disconnect/delete the app data or close the Google account; limited Google backup/deletion cycles may persist.

**Error analysis**

- Data: error message, stack trace, app version, system characteristics and network metadata.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, insofar as no other legal basis is required.
- Recipients: self-operated GlitchTip installation (see section 6).
- Retention: 14 days for error events.

## 5. Data on the device and permissions

For the processes you start, in particular vitamin D estimates and history, supplements, sun exposure, skin type and details of the exposed body area, blood test values, reminders, settings and your OpenWeather API key are processed. This information serves the app function and is not automatically available to all external companies. Data in the app can – depending on the deletion function offered – be removed individually, deleted by resetting the app data or removed by uninstalling. A file you exported yourself remains in the chosen destination directory.

- Exact or approximate location: calculate local UV/weather values at your request.
- Notifications/exact alarms: reminders you have set up.
- Files: export or import your data.

A system permission serves solely the designated device access. It does not replace any further consent for another processing purpose.

## 6. External services and recipients

### Purchases and entitlements (RevenueCat)

To verify purchased Pro unlockings we use RevenueCat; purchase and payment data are processed by the respective app store. [Provider privacy notice](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

### UV/weather retrieval (OpenWeather)

For the UV/weather values you request, coordinates and your API key are transmitted to OpenWeather. [Provider privacy notice](https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy)

### Country lookup

For regional defaults, the first available of the services ipapi.co, FreeIPAPI or ip-api.com is queried. [Provider privacy notice](https://ipapi.co/privacy/)

### Optional Google Drive backup and export

Backup and export go to the Google account you select. [Provider privacy notice](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy)

### Error diagnosis (GlitchTip, self-operated)

To fix errors we use our own GlitchTip installation on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt am Main (Germany, region eu-frankfurt-1). Technical error reports may contain stack traces, app/system versions and connection data. Error events are stored for 14 days; rotated web-server access logs are purged after seven days and maintenance logs after 30 days.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records generally remain until voluntary removal or uninstallation. For network services, retention and deletion periods follow the purpose described in section 4 and, where applicable, the respective recipient's own rules. Uninstalling does not end a subscription obtained via the store and does not automatically delete separately exported files or Drive backups. Where a function was set up with consent, withdrawal is possible for the future. We support the exercise of data-subject rights for data in our area of responsibility at [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).

## 8. Transfers to third countries

The self-operated GlitchTip installation is located in Frankfurt am Main (Germany); for this error data there is no transfer to a third country. RevenueCat, the country-lookup services and Google may also process data outside the EEA; the app stores process payment transactions in their own systems. OpenWeather is based in the United Kingdom. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request information, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, data portability. Processing based on consent can be ended for the future by withdrawing it, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing already carried out. The competent complaints authority includes the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dsb.gv.at/).

We take account of data minimisation and appropriate security measures. No technical transmission is entirely risk-free. Insofar as the product is directed at minors, additional protection and consent requirements apply. We will inform you about material changes to the data processing in an updated policy; any new consents required are not deemed given merely by continued use.
