# Privacy Policy for Memory Palace Training

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Scope

This privacy policy covers **Memory Palace Training** and describes the processing related to flashcards, images and virtual memory palaces. The actually published app version is authoritative. The data described serves the function you trigger.

## 2. Controller

**Marvin Perzi (Persie0)**, Angerdorf 6, 3822 Karlstein an der Thaya, Austria. Email: [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com). Further company details and contact options: [Imprint](https://persie0.github.io/translations_imprint/imprint-de.html).

## 3. At a glance

- Memory palaces, flashcards and training history remain on your device; no user account is required.
- Optional: backup via Google Drive, synchronisation with your local Anki installation and a feedback portal.
- The Android version displays advertising via Appodeal; personalised advertising only takes place after your consent.
- Pro unlockings are billed via the app store and verified via RevenueCat.
- Error reports go to a self-operated GlitchTip installation in Frankfurt am Main.

## 4. Processing by purpose

Processing is organised by function. A distinction must be drawn between purely local processing and data that arises on servers or with independent services. Where processes run exclusively on your device, Persie0 does not automatically gain access to their contents.

**Using the app on your device**

- Data: memory palaces, rooms, objects, mnemonics and selected images; imported lists and training history; settings, consent decisions and synchronisation status
- Purpose: providing the app function on your device.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the data remains on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete the local data, reset it or uninstall the app.

**Display and measure advertising and prevent ad fraud** *(optional; personalised advertising only with consent)*

- Data: advertising ID (where permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad impressions, clicks and fraud-prevention signals, and an approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Legal basis: consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) TKG 2021 (Austrian Telecommunications Act) for non-essential access to end devices. You can change this choice in the app's privacy settings.
- Recipients: Appodeal and its advertising partners (see section 6).
- Retention: the consent choice remains on the device until it is changed, reset or the app is removed; for Appodeal and its partners, the periods stated in their privacy notices apply.

**Enable payment features, restore purchases, prevent fraud and provide support** *(optional)*

- Data: pseudonymous/anonymous app user identifier, product and entitlement identifiers, purchase/subscription status, store transaction metadata, technical app/device data and the IP visible in network logs. Persie0 receives no payment card data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (purchase/service) and Art. 6(1)(f) (fraud prevention and reliable entitlement status).
- Recipients: RevenueCat and the selected app store; the store is independently responsible for payment (see section 6).
- Retention: for the purchase/subscription relationship and as necessary for restoration, disputes and statutory bookkeeping/claim periods; thereafter deletion or de-identification.

**Choosing regional defaults, currency, language or the consent flow**

- Data: public IP address, request metadata and a country/region derived from them; no GPS location.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (minimal regional configuration). Regional settings can be overridden where offered.
- Recipients: whichever country-lookup service is available first (see section 6).
- Retention: the response is used transiently or cached locally; Persie0 keeps no server log. Providers store request logs according to their policies.

**Backing up and synchronising your data on request** *(optional)*

- Data: Google authorisation/account identifier and Drive app-data access; memory palaces, images/paths or bundled assets contained in the selected backup, imported lists, training history, settings and sync metadata.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a)/(b) GDPR; synchronisation is optional and can be disconnected in the app.
- Recipients: Google; the data is held in the Drive account you select (see section 6).
- Retention: until deletion from Drive/app data, disconnection with deletion or closure of the Google account; provider backup cycles may apply.

**Importing/exporting or synchronising learning material on your request** *(optional)*

- Data: deck/card contents, vocabulary/learning fields, tags and sync status selected by the function; local network address when searching for AnkiConnect. The data does not pass through Persie0's servers.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; local device/network access only for the function you want.
- Recipients: your local AnkiDroid/AnkiConnect installation (see section 6).
- Retention: according to the decks/data of your Anki installation; local app sync status until reset/uninstall.

**Capturing feature requests or feedback** *(optional)*

- Data: IP/device/browser data and the name, email, feedback or attachment you enter after opening the external portal.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a)/(b) GDPR for your voluntary request; documentation of support processes Art. 6(1)(f).
- Recipients: Featurebase according to its portal policy; a support copy with us (see section 6).
- Retention: according to the Featurebase policy and until completion; the Persie0 support copy, if needed, for claims for up to three years.

**Answer support requests, investigate errors and document exchanges** *(optional, only if you contact us)*

- Data: sender address, message, attachments and technical email headers.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; Art. 6(1)(f) for support quality and defence of claims.
- Recipients: your email provider, email transport services and Proton (see section 6).
- Retention: for the support exchange and, if needed, for legal claims for up to three years afterwards; longer only under a legal obligation.

**Technical error diagnosis**

- Data: app/system version, error message, stack trace and technical HTTP/device data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, protection against errors and abuse.
- Recipients: self-operated GlitchTip installation (see section 6).
- Retention: 14 days for error events.

## 5. On-device processing and permissions

The device storage is used in particular for the following data for the functions you request: memory palaces, rooms, objects, mnemonics and selected images, imported lists and training history, settings, consent decisions and synchronisation status. You can remove stored content via the app function offered for that purpose or reset local app data via the operating system. Exported copies may continue to exist outside the app.

- Camera/photos/files: add images or import/export material.
- Local network or AnkiDroid database: optional Anki synchronisation.
- Exact location: may be requested under Android, but is not required for the main functions and can be denied.
- Network: optional synchronisation, feedback, advertising, diagnostics and purchases.

A system permission only allows the respective device access and is not consent to independent advertising or analytics purposes.

## 6. External services and recipients

### Advertising (Appodeal)

For the delivery of advertisements, the Android version integrates Appodeal and the following mediation and provider networks: [Appodeal](https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/), [AppLovin](https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/), [BidMachine](https://www.bidmachine.com/privacy-policy), **Bidon** (ad mediation), [BIGO Ads](https://www.adsbigo.com/privacy.html), [DT Exchange](https://www.digitalturbine.com/legal), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [Mintegral](https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy/), [Unity Ads](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Vungle](https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/). Depending on location, your privacy decision, ad format and the specific ad auction, not every provider is addressed on every ad request. Section 4 describes which data is processed on which legal basis; the providers' own purposes, countries and retention rules can be found in their linked privacy notices.

### Purchases and entitlements (RevenueCat)

To verify purchased unlockings we use RevenueCat; purchase and payment data are processed by the respective app store. [Provider privacy notice](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

### Country lookup

For regional defaults, the first available of the services ipapi.co, FreeIPAPI or ip-api.com is queried. [Provider privacy notice](https://ipapi.co/privacy/)

### Optional Google Drive synchronisation

Backup and synchronisation go to the Google Drive account you select. [Provider privacy notice](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy)

### Optional Anki integration

Anki synchronisation runs against your local AnkiDroid/AnkiConnect installation; any further AnkiWeb synchronisation is controlled by you and Anki. [Provider privacy notice](https://docs.ankidroid.org/privacy_policy.html)

### Optional feedback portal

Feedback is collected via Featurebase, which processes it under its own portal policy. [Provider privacy notice](https://www.featurebase.app/legal/privacy)

### Support by email

If you contact us, your own email provider is also involved in the transmission. On our side we use Proton Mail ([privacy notice](https://proton.me/legal/privacy)).

### Error reports (GlitchTip, self-operated)

To fix program errors we use our own GlitchTip installation on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt am Main (Germany, region eu-frankfurt-1). The app and system version, error messages, stack traces and technical connection data may be processed. Error events are stored for 14 days; rotated web-server access logs are purged after seven days and technical maintenance logs after 30 days.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Resetting app data or uninstalling removes the content stored on the device; copies you exported yourself are unaffected.

## 8. Transfers to third countries

The self-operated GlitchTip installation is located in Frankfurt am Main (Germany); for this error data there is no transfer to a third country. Appodeal and the provider networks involved, RevenueCat, Google (Drive), Featurebase and email services may also process data outside the EEA, for example with providers based in the USA. Anki synchronisation runs locally against your own installation. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, where applicable data portability and objection. Consents given can be withdrawn for the future. You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, in particular the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://dsb.gv.at/). For matters addressed to us, contact [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).

## 10. Children, security and updates

We align processing with the respective purpose and take appropriate security measures. The relevant protective provisions apply to minors. If the purposes, data types or recipients change materially, we will adapt this policy and, where necessary, obtain separate consent.
