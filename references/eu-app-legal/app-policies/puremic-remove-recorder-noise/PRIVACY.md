# Privacy Policy for PureMic: Remove Recorder Noise

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Scope

This privacy policy covers **PureMic: Remove Recorder Noise** and describes the processing related to recording, importing and cleaning audio files. The actually published app version is authoritative. The data described serves the function you trigger.

## 2. Controller

**Marvin Perzi (Persie0)**, Angerdorf 6, 3822 Karlstein an der Thaya, Austria. Email: [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com). Further company details and contact options: [Imprint](https://persie0.github.io/translations_imprint/imprint-de.html).

## 3. At a glance

- Recordings and imported audio files are cleaned locally on your device; your audio data is not uploaded. No user account is required.
- The Android version displays advertising via Appodeal; personalised advertising only takes place after your consent.
- Pro unlockings are billed via the app store and verified via RevenueCat.
- A country-lookup service is queried for regional settings.
- Error reports go to a self-operated GlitchTip installation in Frankfurt am Main.

## 4. Processing by purpose

Processing is organised by function. A distinction must be drawn between purely local processing and data that arises on servers or with independent services. Where processes run exclusively on your device, Persie0 does not automatically gain access to their contents.

**Using the app on your device**

- Data: recordings and imported audio files; processed audio files and temporary files; history, settings and consent decisions
- Purpose: providing the app function on your device.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the data remains on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete the local data, reset it or uninstall the app.

**Display and measure advertising and prevent ad fraud** *(optional; personalised advertising only with consent)*

- Data: advertising ID (where permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad impressions, clicks and fraud-prevention signals, and an approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Legal basis: consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) TKG 2021 (Austrian Telecommunications Act) for non-essential access to end devices. You can change this choice in the app's privacy settings.
- Recipients: Appodeal and its advertising partners (see section 6).
- Retention: the consent choice remains on the device until it is changed, reset or the app is removed; for Appodeal and its partners, the periods stated in their privacy notices apply.

**Enable payment features, restore purchases, prevent fraud and provide support** *(optional)*

- Data: pseudonymous/anonymous app user identifier, product and entitlement identifiers, purchase/subscription status, store transaction metadata, technical app/device data and the IP visible in network logs. Persie0 receives no payment card data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (purchase/service) and Art. 6(1)(f) (fraud prevention and reliable entitlement status).
- Recipients: RevenueCat and the selected app store; the store is independently responsible for payment (see section 6).
- Retention: for the purchase/subscription relationship and as necessary for restoration, disputes and statutory bookkeeping/claim periods; thereafter deletion or de-identification.

**Choosing regional defaults, currency, language or the consent flow**

- Data: public IP address, request metadata and a country/region derived from them; no GPS location.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (minimal regional configuration). Regional settings can be overridden where offered.
- Recipients: whichever country-lookup service is available first (see section 6).
- Retention: the response is used transiently or cached locally; Persie0 keeps no server log. Providers store request logs according to their policies.

**Technical error diagnosis**

- Data: app/system version, error message, stack trace and technical HTTP/device data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, protection against errors and abuse.
- Recipients: self-operated GlitchTip installation (see section 6).
- Retention: 14 days for error events.

## 5. On-device processing and permissions

The device storage is used in particular for the following data for the functions you request: recordings and imported audio files, processed audio files and temporary files, history, settings and consent decisions. You can remove stored content via the app function offered for that purpose or reset local app data via the operating system. Exported copies may continue to exist outside the app.

- Microphone: record the audio you want.
- Files/media: select or save audio.
- Network: country lookup, advertising, diagnostics and purchases.

A system permission only allows the respective device access and is not consent to independent advertising or analytics purposes.

For the delivery of advertisements, the Android version integrates Appodeal and the following mediation and provider networks: [Appodeal](https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/), [AppLovin](https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/), [BidMachine](https://www.bidmachine.com/privacy-policy), **Bidon** (ad mediation), [BIGO Ads](https://www.adsbigo.com/privacy.html), [DT Exchange](https://www.digitalturbine.com/legal), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [Mintegral](https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy/), [Unity Ads](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Vungle](https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/). Section 4 describes which data is processed on which legal basis; the providers' own purposes, countries and retention rules can be found in their linked privacy notices.

### Purchases and entitlements (RevenueCat)

To verify purchased unlockings we use RevenueCat; purchase and payment data are processed by the respective app store. [Provider privacy notice](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

### Country lookup

For regional defaults, the first available of the services ipapi.co, FreeIPAPI or ip-api.com is queried. [Provider privacy notice](https://ipapi.co/privacy/)

### Error reports (GlitchTip, self-operated)

To fix program errors we use our own GlitchTip installation on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt am Main (Germany, region eu-frankfurt-1). The app and system version, error messages, stack traces and technical connection data may be processed. Error events are stored for 14 days; rotated web-server access logs are purged after seven days and technical maintenance logs after 30 days.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Resetting app data or uninstalling removes the content stored on the device; copies you exported yourself are unaffected.

## 8. Transfers to third countries

The self-operated GlitchTip installation is located in Frankfurt am Main (Germany); for this error data there is no transfer to a third country. Appodeal and the provider networks involved, RevenueCat and the country-lookup service may also process data outside the EEA, for example with providers based in the USA. App stores may also store data outside the EEA. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.
