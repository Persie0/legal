# Privacy Policy for UniMinder – Uni Companion

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Scope

This privacy policy covers **UniMinder** and describes the processing related to calendar, course, exam, alarm and university portal functions. The app is not an official service of TU Wien. For TISS, TUWEL and, where applicable, Microsoft, the respective provider notices apply in addition. The actually published app version is authoritative.

## 3. At a glance

- Appointments, reminders and university and session information remain on your device; no user account with Persie0 is required.
- University and calendar data are only loaded when you actively connect via the TU Wien endpoints (TISS, TUWEL) or a service you select.
- In the reviewed version, no advertising and no payment service are integrated.
- Error reports may be sent to Sentry.io.

## 4. Processing by purpose

Processing is organised by function. A distinction must be drawn between purely local processing and data that arises on servers or with independent services. Where processes run exclusively on your device, Persie0 does not automatically gain access to their contents.

**Using the app on your device**

- Data: university and session information; appointments; reminders
- Purpose: providing the app function on your device.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the data remains on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete the local data, reset it or uninstall the app.

**Loading university and calendar data at your request** *(optional, only with an active connection)*

- Data: university logins and session tokens you select yourself, course/calendar data and appointments; where a Microsoft or university connection is used, the data transmitted to the respective service.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the university and calendar functions you request; the services concerned are independently responsible for their processing.
- Recipients: TU Wien endpoints (TISS, TUWEL) and the service you select (see section 6).
- Retention: locally until deletion; the university and Microsoft services are subject to their own policies.

**Technical error diagnosis**

- Data: error message, stack trace, app version, system characteristics and network metadata.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, insofar as no other legal basis is required.
- Recipients: Sentry.io (Functional Software, Inc.; see section 6).
- Retention: according to the retention rules agreed with Sentry and the statutory data-protection requirements.

## 5. On-device processing and permissions

The device storage is used in particular for the following data for the functions you request: university and session information, appointments and reminders. You can remove stored content via the app function offered for that purpose or reset local app data via the operating system. Exported copies may continue to exist outside the app.

For the functions offered, no blanket access rights to all files or sensors are requested.

A system permission only allows the respective device access and is not consent to independent advertising or analytics purposes.

## 6. External services and recipients

### University and calendar services

With an active connection, logins, session tokens, course/calendar data and appointments are loaded via the relevant TU Wien endpoints (TISS, TUWEL). If you use a Microsoft or university connection, data is transmitted to the service you select; the providers' own notices apply to these services. [Microsoft privacy notice](https://privacy.microsoft.com/)

### Error reports (Sentry.io)

To fix program errors a technical report may be sent to Sentry.io (Functional Software, Inc.); this can include error messages, stack traces, app and operating-system versions and technical connection and device information. Retention and possible transfers outside the EEA are governed by the retention rules agreed with Sentry and the statutory data-protection requirements. [Sentry privacy notice](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Resetting app data or uninstalling removes the content stored on the device; copies you exported yourself are unaffected.

## 8. Transfers to third countries

With an active connection, data is transmitted to the TU Wien endpoints (TISS, TUWEL) and, where applicable, to the service you select such as Microsoft; their responsibility and choice of country follow the respective provider notices, and Microsoft may also process data outside the EEA. Error reports may be sent to Sentry.io; processing outside the EEA may occur. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, where applicable data portability and objection. Consents given can be withdrawn for the future. You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, in particular the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://dsb.gv.at/). For matters addressed to us, contact [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).

## 10. Children, security and updates

We align processing with the respective purpose and take appropriate security measures. The relevant protective provisions apply to minors. If the purposes, data types or recipients change materially, we will adapt this policy and, where necessary, obtain separate consent.
