# Privacy Policy – Resistor Finder

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Who does this policy apply to?

This policy applies to **Resistor Finder**, an application for calculating resistance values from colour-code inputs. In the reviewed version, paid features are purchased via the app store's native purchase libraries, not via RevenueCat. For external websites or voluntarily connected online accounts, the operators' own privacy notices may additionally apply.

## 3. At a glance

- The colour-ring calculation runs on your device; inputs and results remain stored locally.
- No user account is required.
- The Android version displays advertising via Appodeal; personalised advertising only takes place after your consent.
- Voluntary unlockings are billed via the app store; payment instruments are processed by the store.
- Error reports go to a self-operated GlitchTip installation in Frankfurt am Main.

## 4. Processing by purpose

We distinguish between information processed exclusively in the private storage of your device and information that arises with network services. Signing in to an app store does not automatically mean that Persie0 receives all data stored by that store.

**Local app function**

- Data: selected colour rings and calculation values; settings and recent calculations.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the processing takes place on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete it, reset the app data or uninstall.

**Display and measure advertising and prevent ad fraud** *(optional; personalised advertising only with consent)*

- Data: advertising ID (where permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad impressions, clicks and fraud-prevention signals, and an approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Legal basis: consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) TKG 2021 (Austrian Telecommunications Act) for non-essential access to end devices. You can change this choice in the app's privacy settings.
- Recipients: Appodeal and its advertising partners (see section 6).
- Retention: the consent choice remains on the device until it is changed, reset or the app is removed; for Appodeal and its partners, the periods stated in their privacy notices apply.

**Answer support requests, investigate errors and document exchanges** *(optional, only if you contact us)*

- Data: sender address, message, attachments and technical email headers.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; Art. 6(1)(f) for support quality and defence of claims.
- Recipients: your email provider, email transport services and Proton (see section 6).
- Retention: for the support exchange and, if needed, for legal claims for up to three years afterwards; longer only under a legal obligation.

**Processing voluntary in-app purchases** *(optional)*

- Data: store product, purchase status and entitlement identifier; no payment card data arises at Persie0.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as the purchase status is needed for the acquisition.
- Recipients: the respective app store (Apple or Google), which is independently responsible for payment.
- Retention: according to the store's terms and until the local entitlement is deleted or reset.

**Error analysis**

- Data: error message, stack trace, app version, system characteristics and network metadata.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, insofar as no other legal basis is required.
- Recipients: self-operated GlitchTip installation (see section 6).
- Retention: 14 days for error events.

## 5. Data on the device and permissions

For the processes you start, in particular the selected colour rings and calculation values as well as settings and recent calculations are processed. This information serves the app function and is not automatically available to all external companies. Data in the app can – depending on the deletion function offered – be removed individually, deleted by resetting the app data or removed by uninstalling. A file you exported yourself remains in the chosen destination directory.

- Network: connection for advertising, diagnostics and purchases.
- Email app, only if you choose to send a report: send a support message.

A system permission serves solely the designated device access. It does not replace any further consent for another processing purpose.

For the delivery of advertisements, the Android version integrates Appodeal and the following mediation and provider networks: [Appodeal](https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/), [AppLovin](https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/), [BidMachine](https://www.bidmachine.com/privacy-policy), **Bidon** (ad mediation), [BIGO Ads](https://www.adsbigo.com/privacy.html), [DT Exchange](https://www.digitalturbine.com/legal), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [Mintegral](https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy/), [Unity Ads](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Vungle](https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/). Section 4 describes which data is processed on which legal basis; the providers' own purposes, countries and retention rules can be found in their linked privacy notices.

### Support by email

If you contact us, your own email provider is also involved in the transmission. On our side we use Proton Mail ([privacy notice](https://proton.me/legal/privacy)).

### Purchases via Apple or Google

The purchase price and payment data are processed by the respective app store; the app only uses the purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock features. [Apple privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) · [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Error diagnosis (GlitchTip, self-operated)

To fix program errors we use our own GlitchTip installation on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt am Main (Germany, region eu-frankfurt-1). Error descriptions, stack traces, app/system versions and technical connection data may be processed. Error events are stored for 14 days; rotated web-server access logs are purged after seven days and technical maintenance logs after 30 days.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records generally remain until voluntary removal or uninstallation. For network services, retention and deletion periods follow the purpose described in section 4 and, where applicable, the respective recipient's own rules. Uninstalling does not end a subscription obtained via the store and does not automatically delete separately exported files or backups. Where a function was set up with consent, withdrawal is possible for the future. We support the exercise of data-subject rights for data in our area of responsibility at [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).

## 8. Transfers to third countries

The self-operated GlitchTip installation is located in Frankfurt am Main (Germany); for this error data there is no transfer to a third country. Appodeal and the provider networks involved may also process data outside the EEA, for example with providers based in the USA. App stores and email services may also store data outside the EEA. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request information, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, data portability. Processing based on consent can be ended for the future by withdrawing it, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing already carried out. The competent complaints authority includes the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dsb.gv.at/).

## 10. Data security, children and updates

We take account of data minimisation and appropriate security measures. No technical transmission is entirely risk-free. Insofar as the product is directed at minors, additional protection and consent requirements apply. We will inform you about material changes to the data processing in an updated policy; any new consents required are not deemed given merely by continued use.
