**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

This privacy policy covers **Pareader** and describes the processing related to e-books, RSS feeds, vocabulary and requested AI functions. The actually published app version is authoritative. The data described serves the function you trigger.

## 3. At a glance

- Books, RSS articles, bookmarks, vocabulary and private shadowing recordings remain on your device; no user account is required.

- In the reviewed version, no advertising and no payment service are integrated.
- Error reports may be sent to Sentry.io.

## 4. Processing by purpose

Processing is organised by function. A distinction must be drawn between purely local processing and data that arises on servers or with independent services. Where processes run exclusively on your device, Persie0 does not automatically gain access to their contents.

**Using the app on your device**

- Data: books, EPUBs, saved articles, RSS feeds, bookmarks and reading history; vocabulary, exercises, learning progress and Anki synchronisation status; private shadowing recordings; Google AI and NVIDIA API keys in the secure operating-system storage
- Purpose: providing the app function on your device.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the data remains on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete the local data, reset it or uninstall the app.

**Providing the AI function you expressly start** *(optional, with your own API key)*

- Data: Google AI or NVIDIA API key and the words, sentences, article or book texts, transcripts, inputs and generated content selected for the function. Translations use Google AI; for other AI actions NVIDIA may be selected. The API keys are held in the secure operating-system storage and are not sent to Persie0.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a)/(b) GDPR; Google or NVIDIA act under your API account/contract.
- Recipients: Google AI or NVIDIA (see section 6).
- Retention: Persie0 stores locally only the outputs you save. Retention and any training depend on the paid service and account settings; check the current terms before entering personal data.

**Opening or retrieving the content you want**

- Data: IP, user agent, request time, requested URL/content and cookies/login data controlled by the external service. Selected text goes to the AI service only for an AI action you start.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for your requested action; the external service is independently responsible.
- Recipients: the operator of the respective website or RSS feed (see section 6).
- Retention: according to the destination's privacy/cookie settings; Persie0 stores only the history/bookmarks you save locally.

**Importing/exporting or synchronising learning material on your request** *(optional)*

- Data: deck/card contents, vocabulary/learning fields, tags and sync status selected by the function; local network address when searching for AnkiConnect. The data does not pass through Persie0's servers.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; local device/network access only for the function you want.
- Recipients: your local AnkiDroid/AnkiConnect installation (see section 6).
- Retention: according to the decks/data of your Anki installation; local app sync status until reset/uninstall.

**Answer support requests, investigate errors and document exchanges** *(optional, only if you contact us)*

- Data: sender address, message, attachments and technical email headers.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; Art. 6(1)(f) for support quality and defence of claims.
- Recipients: your email provider, email transport services and Proton (see section 6).
- Retention: for the support exchange and, if needed, for legal claims for up to three years afterwards; longer only under a legal obligation.

**Technical error diagnosis**

- Data: app/system version, error message, stack trace and technical HTTP/device data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, protection against errors and abuse.
- Recipients: Sentry.io (Functional Software, Inc.; see section 6).
- Retention: according to the retention rules agreed with Sentry and the statutory data-protection requirements.

You can remove stored content via the app function offered for that purpose or reset local app data via the operating system. Exported copies may continue to exist outside the app.

[Sentry privacy notice](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Resetting app data or uninstalling removes the content stored on the device; copies you exported yourself are unaffected.

The API keys remain on your device and are not sent to Persie0. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, in particular the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://dsb.gv.at/). For matters addressed to us, contact [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).
