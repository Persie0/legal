# Privacy Policy for Toilet Compass: WC Finder

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Scope

This privacy policy covers **Toilet Compass** and describes the processing related to searching for toilets based on location and compass. The device location, the download of the regional toilet dataset and the network access to a map tile are different technical processes. The actually published app version is authoritative.

## 2. Controller

**Marvin Perzi (Persie0)**, Angerdorf 6, 3822 Karlstein an der Thaya, Austria. Email: [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com). Further company details and contact options: [Imprint](https://persie0.github.io/translations_imprint/imprint-de.html).

## 3. At a glance

- Toilets, location and compass data remain on your device; no user account is required.
- Regional offline databases are loaded via GitHub; the download URL contains no live GPS coordinate.
- Map tiles are loaded from CARTO based on OpenStreetMap data.
- On request, a route is opened in the map app of your choice; its provider is then responsible.
- Error reports go to a self-operated GlitchTip installation in Frankfurt am Main.

## 4. Processing by purpose

Processing is organised by function. A distinction must be drawn between purely local processing and data that arises on servers or with independent services. Where processes run exclusively on your device, Persie0 does not automatically gain access to their contents.

**Using the app on your device**

- Data: downloaded regional toilet databases; current location and compass direction during use; cached matches and settings
- Purpose: providing the app function on your device.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the data remains on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete the local data, reset it or uninstall the app.

**Loading the selected offline database and verifying its integrity**

- Data: IP, request metadata and the requested continent database; the download URL contains no live GPS coordinate.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Art. 6(1)(f) for secure distribution.
- Recipients: GitHub (see section 6).
- Retention: the database remains on the device until removal/uninstallation; GitHub stores download logs according to its policy, Persie0 keeps no separate log.

**Displaying the map area you want**

- Data: IP, user agent, request time and the requested map tile coordinates/zoom level, from which the area viewed can be inferred. The map provider receives no Persie0 account password.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the requested map and Art. 6(1)(f) for efficient delivery.
- Recipients: CARTO/CartoCDN and, for direct queries, OpenStreetMap-related services (see section 6).
- Retention: Persie0 keeps no map tile request logs; provider periods apply. The local cache is deleted by the app/operating system or uninstallation.

**Opening a route at your request** *(optional)*

- Data: destination as well as the current location/context the selected map app adds after you open it; IP and device/account data according to its settings.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; the selected external provider is independently responsible.
- Recipients: the map app/website you select (see section 6).
- Retention: according to the external provider's account/privacy settings; Persie0 does not receive the route.

**Technical error diagnosis**

- Data: app/system version, error message, stack trace and technical HTTP/device data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, protection against errors and abuse.
- Recipients: self-operated GlitchTip installation (see section 6).
- Retention: 14 days for error events.

## 5. On-device processing and permissions

The device storage is used in particular for the following data for the functions you request: downloaded regional toilet databases, current location and compass direction during use, cached matches and settings. You can remove stored content via the app function offered for that purpose or reset local app data via the operating system. Exported copies may continue to exist outside the app.

- Exact/approximate location: find and sort nearby toilets.
- Compass/motion sensors: point in the direction of a toilet.
- Network: load toilet databases, map tiles and diagnostics.

A system permission only allows the respective device access and is not consent to independent advertising or analytics purposes.

## 6. External services and recipients

### Toilet databases (GitHub Releases)

The regional offline databases are provided via GitHub Releases; IP address, request time and user agent may be processed. [Provider privacy notice](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

### Map tiles (CARTO/CartoCDN)

For the map display, tiles are loaded via CARTO based on OpenStreetMap data. [Provider privacy notice](https://carto.com/privacy/)

### External route guidance

At your request, the app opens the destination toilet in the map app or website you select (such as Google Maps); the respective provider governs its data use. [Google privacy notice](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Error reports (GlitchTip, self-operated)

To fix program errors we use our own GlitchTip installation on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt am Main (Germany, region eu-frankfurt-1). The app and system version, error messages, stack traces and technical connection data may be processed. Error events are stored for 14 days; rotated web-server access logs are purged after seven days and technical maintenance logs after 30 days.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Resetting app data or uninstalling removes the content stored on the device; copies you exported yourself are unaffected.

## 8. Transfers to third countries

The self-operated GlitchTip installation is located in Frankfurt am Main (Germany); for this error data there is no transfer to a third country. GitHub and CARTO may also process data outside the EEA, for example with providers based in the USA. When a route is opened in an external map app, the respective provider's processing applies, which may also take place outside the EEA. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, where applicable data portability and objection. Consents given can be withdrawn for the future. You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, in particular the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://dsb.gv.at/). For matters addressed to us, contact [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).

## 10. Children, security and updates

We align processing with the respective purpose and take appropriate security measures. The relevant protective provisions apply to minors. If the purposes, data types or recipients change materially, we will adapt this policy and, where necessary, obtain separate consent.
