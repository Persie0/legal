# Privacy Policy for Live Noise Cancellation Demo

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Scope

This privacy policy covers **Live Noise Cancellation Demo** and describes the processing related to live processing of microphone audio. The actually published app version is authoritative. Acoustic recording or playback is processed on the device; where external functions are added, they are described separately here.

## 3. At a glance

- The microphone signal is processed live on your device and not sent to our servers.
- The Android version displays advertising via Appodeal; personalised advertising only takes place after your consent.
- Optionally, an external tone generator can be opened in the browser; no microphone audio is transmitted there either.
- Error reports may be sent to Sentry.io.

## 4. Processing by purpose

Processing is organised by function. A distinction must be drawn between purely local processing and data that arises on servers or with independent services. Where processes run exclusively on your device, Persie0 does not automatically gain access to their contents.

**Using the app on your device**

- Data: live microphone buffers processed in working memory; audio and app settings; consent decisions
- Purpose: providing the app function on your device.
- Legal basis: function you requested; where we process personal data ourselves, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Recipients: none; the data remains on your device.
- Retention/deletion: until you delete the local data, reset it or uninstall the app.

**Display and measure advertising and prevent ad fraud** *(optional; personalised advertising only with consent)*

- Data: advertising ID (where permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad impressions, clicks and fraud-prevention signals, and an approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Legal basis: consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) TKG 2021 (Austrian Telecommunications Act) for non-essential access to end devices. You can change this choice in the app's privacy settings.
- Recipients: Appodeal and its advertising partners (see section 6).
- Retention: the consent choice remains on the device until it is changed, reset or the app is removed; for Appodeal and its partners, the periods stated in their privacy notices apply.

**Opening an optional external reference/tool** *(optional)*

- Data: IP, browser/device data and any cookies of the website after you choose to open it; microphone audio is not sent by the app.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for your requested action; the destination operator is independently responsible.
- Recipients: the operator of the website opened (see section 6).
- Retention: according to the destination page's policy; Persie0 receives no website activity.

**Technical error diagnosis**

- Data: app/system version, error message, stack trace and technical HTTP/device data.
- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, protection against errors and abuse.
- Recipients: Sentry.io (Functional Software, Inc.; see section 6).
- Retention: according to the retention rules agreed with Sentry and the statutory data-protection requirements.

## 5. On-device processing and permissions

The device storage is used in particular for the following data for the functions you request: live microphone buffers processed in working memory, audio and app settings, consent decisions. You can remove stored content via the app function offered for that purpose or reset local app data via the operating system. Exported copies may continue to exist outside the app.

- Microphone/audio settings: provide live processing.
- Network: advertising, diagnostics and an external tone generator you optionally open.

A system permission only allows the respective device access and is not consent to independent advertising or analytics purposes.

Retention and possible transfers outside the EEA are governed by the retention rules agreed with Sentry and the statutory data-protection requirements. [Sentry privacy notice](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Resetting app data or uninstalling removes the content stored on the device; copies you exported yourself are unaffected.

## 8. Transfers to third countries

Appodeal and the provider networks involved may also process data outside the EEA, for example with providers based in the USA. The optionally opened tone generator and Sentry.io may also process data outside the EEA; microphone audio is not transmitted. Insofar as the GDPR applies, such transfers are based on a permissible ground under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or suitable contractual safeguards.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, where applicable data portability and objection. Consents given can be withdrawn for the future. You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, in particular the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://dsb.gv.at/). For matters addressed to us, contact [persie0@protonmail.com](mailto:persie0@protonmail.com).

## 10. Children, security and updates

We align processing with the respective purpose and take appropriate security measures. The relevant protective provisions apply to minors. If the purposes, data types or recipients change materially, we will adapt this policy and, where necessary, obtain separate consent.
