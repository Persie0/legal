## What is already handled by other providers

Apple's default App Store EULA may suffice without a custom licence. StoreKit/Google Play manage native transactions, subscription billing and store-side cancellation/refunds; they do **not** write your privacy policy, explain app-specific entitlements or fulfill your separate GDPR duties. Appodeal's Stack Consent Manager can handle **advertising choices** when correctly configured; it does not provide a complete app privacy policy or substitute for ATT, independent health-data consent or disclosure of other SDKs.

For straightforward offline games and utilities, **terms drafts are optional reference text**, not a statement that a bespoke EULA or mandatory acceptance screen is legally required.

## Before publishing

1. Check the exact **shipped store build** (including activated mediation and runtime data transfers), not only the repository HEAD.
2. Confirm each service's actual recipient, GDPR basis, retention/deletion criteria, transfer mechanism and any special-category data.
3. Fill/remove all **[VOR VERÖFFENTLICHUNG]** markers; remove the separate editorial sections at the end.
4. Ensure German/English translations and in-app permission/CMP screens reflect the real behavior, with separate opt-ins where required.
5. For purchases, confirm product/trial/renewal price, merchant/contract party, cancellations, statutory withdrawals and a compliant online withdrawal flow if § 13a FAGG applies.
6. Publish valid HTML privacy URLs and configure both stores' privacy metadata and required account-deletion URLs. A raw GitHub Markdown page does **not** automatically replace these fields.
