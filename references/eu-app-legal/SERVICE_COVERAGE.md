# Coverage audit: references for services and data used by our apps

**Audit date:** 16 October 2026  
**Compared against:** [SERVICES_AND_DATA.md](../../SERVICES_AND_DATA.md) (current repository code, not necessarily store builds).  
**Registry:** [sources.json](sources.json) (external Austrian/EU publisher references).

**Result:** All **16 named integration categories** in the service inventory have at least one relevant legal-document reference (publisher precedent **or** direct provider documentation). That **does not mean** each app's privacy policy/EULA is complete or every SDK/ad-network recipient is verified. See limitations and open items below.

There are two different kinds of references:

- **EU/Austrian app publisher precedents**: examples of what other apps disclose for similar features. They do **not** prove that they use Appodeal, RevenueCat, GlitchTip or any specific SDK. Never copy their statements into your policies.
- **Actual provider policies/SDK requirements**: primary sources for **the services your code integrates**. These take priority over competitors when identifying data recipients, SDK data collection, consent, data processing, or international transfers.

## Priority unresolved items before claiming full legal coverage

1. **Ad mediation — highest priority.** For every current release build, enumerate all resolved/transitive ad SDKs and their exact privacy policies/recipients; compare active consent flows, location permissions, device IDs and ATT before publishing Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data safety statements.
2. **Self-hosted crash reports.** Record where your GlitchTip instance actually runs, subprocessor/hosting agreement, data retention, inbound fields and scrubber behavior. The GlitchTip vendor's SaaS privacy policy does **not** replace your privacy disclosure.
3. **Unknown runtime endpoints.** Check conditional `SENTRY_DSN` build settings and the translation service instance; an unverified endpoint cannot be treated as a known processor.
4. **Cloud data categories.** Inventory specific Google Drive/iCloud fields and backups per app (especially Vitamin D Tracker health-related logs), encryption and user deletion.
5. **AI APIs.** Confirm whether prompts or uploaded user media reach OpenAI/Gemini; distinguish EEA-specific Gemini terms and billed vs unbilled/API modes, and obtain required data processing terms.
6. **University authentication.** Verify exact TU Wien TISS/TUWEL/Microsoft policies and which auth tokens and calendar contents UniMinder stores/transmits.
7. **Missing source for two apps.** Selfie Diary and VPN & Proxy Detector were not matched to their code in the earlier audit; their actual SDK/data inventory requires code review before legal certification.
8. **Per-app legal artifacts.** A matched peer privacy policy is not proof that **your** app has a published privacy policy, terms/EULA, impressum, consent UI, store metadata, or contractual basis. That is a separate audit per app and platform.
