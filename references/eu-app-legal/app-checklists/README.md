## How to use these documents

1. Read the app file, which includes its code evidence, concrete data categories, recipients, app-specific legal sections, store/consent/consumer requirements and verification checkboxes.
2. Verify the **specific release binary** against source; independently confirm which SDK/ad adapters are active and what actually leaves the device.
3. Draft each app's own public privacy page and needed terms in clear German/English, using verified factual data, retention schedules, lawful bases and provider names.
4. Keep the common legally correct Austrian **Impressum** across apps. Link app-specific privacy URLs and terms/consent management from within each app and store listing.
5. Match Play Data safety/Health declaration and Apple's App Privacy, ATT/permissions/trader disclosures to the same facts. A custom EULA is optional where Apple's default is sufficient.
6. Do not replace optional advertising consent with a generic licence checkbox. Appodeal's CMP can collect **ad** consent but does not replace app-specific GDPR notice, all partner disclosures, access/deletion, sensitive-data consent or store privacy forms.

**Evidence limitation:** The two files for **Selfie Diary** and **VPN & Proxy Detector** are explicit placeholders for a future source audit. They contain reasonable *conditional* requirements based on app title, not invented SDK facts.

## Priority areas to validate

- **Special-category health data:** Vitamin D Tracker — Art. 9 basis, explicit consent (where relied on), cloud backup, medical claims.
- **User accounts/social sharing:** NomadRank — actual account deletion and retention, friends/groups data, visibility, content rights and DSA analysis.
- **Broad ad mediation and location:** Fake Location Detector, Root Detection — actual adapter/identifier data, consent withdrawal and store disclosures.
- **Media and facial data:** Noise Remover, PureMic, video/image upscalers, 3D Face Model Maker, Selfie Diary — local versus uploaded content, file caches, output retention and possible biometric processing.
- **Cloud/AI/university accounts:** Memory Palace, Yoyillo Chinese, Pareader, UniMinder — exact scopes, endpoints, data disclosure and erasure.
- **Subscriptions and paid trials:** RevenueCat/native IAP apps — real purchase options, trial renewal, cancellation and applicable Austrian consumer withdrawal rights.
