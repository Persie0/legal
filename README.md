## Release-status notes

- **Google Play:** 41 app entries were shown; 31 are marked Production. The other 10 are drafts, internal tests, rejected, removed, or suspended and are not counted as current production entries.
- **App Store Connect:** *Prepare for Submission*, *Ready for Review*, and *Rejected* describe the listed version's status, **not necessarily** whether an earlier version remains live. The four iOS and one macOS entries above are the only explicitly *Ready for Distribution* versions in the supplied list.
